# Progress against our quarterly objectives

## Digital Experience
### Highlights
The second slice of the image sidebar preview went public on 18 November, so the sidebar can now be dragged wider to see more of a picture, and the third slice, stepping from image to image without returning to the results, is largely built, with its small-screen version still in review. From 13 November the largest archives, such as the Francis Crick papers, open with only their top level showing and load each folder when opened, rather than 50 rows at a time. The team also agreed how new features will reach a growing share of visitors first. ([13537](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13537), [13782](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13782), [13786](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13786))

### Deliver a faster, friction-free image browsing and viewing experience
- Next and previous buttons, the arrow keys and the browser’s Back button now move through results inside the sidebar, ready for the third slice. Screen readers now announce each image as you move, which the first build did not do reliably. ([13874](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13874), [13876](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13876), [13897](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13897))
- Image results can now appear as a masonry grid, each picture at its own shape with its title underneath, not yet public. ([13535](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13535))
- Analytics now record sidebar resizing and stepping, and no longer count scrolling again every time an image opens. ([13838](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13838), [13887](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13887))
- Next and previous navigation is now added to the sidebar ([13536](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13536))

### Improve our agility to get working changes in front of users sooner
- The team settled how a feature will reach a share of visitors first. Each feature has its own audience, the share only rises, and people already included stay in. ([13786](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13786), [13785](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13785))
- Staff can now switch off a feature that is already public, for themselves only, to compare it with the old version. ([13832](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13832), [13833](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13833))

### Other
- The two iPads at the library entrance now use the website’s kiosk mode, so visitors only see links that work, idle screens return to the collections page and use is counted. ([13819](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13819))
- Crawlers hitting the staff preview copy of the website hundreds of thousands of times a day are now sent to the live site, easing load on the image service. ([13818](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13818))
- Our own website firewall was mistaking catalogue filter links such as “Keys (Legends)” for attacks and refused 311 searches in 30 days; the fix went live on 24 November. ([705](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform-infrastructure/issues/705))

## Digital Platform
### Highlights
An end to end rehearsal of moving patron accounts from Sierra to FOLIO, on test accounts that kept their card numbers, caught a fault that would have stopped every login at cutover. The new catalogue built from FOLIO was loaded in full for the first time. And manuscripts now show their binding, decoration and condition on the website. ([6870](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6870), [6585](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6585), [6818](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6818))

### Folio works and items are transformed into the catalogue's Work model and merged correctly
- The load holds all 1,039,096 FOLIO records, for staff only. About 38,000 digitised items lost the Sierra record joining them to their archive record; the Axiell sync should restore most. ([6585](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6585))
- Items and holdings now match Sierra, including journal runs in order, ordered material shown as on order, and long missing items shown as unavailable. ([6611](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6611), [6798](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6798), [6873](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6873))
- Notes, identifiers such as ISBNs, and format now come across, as the sprint goal planned. ([6567](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6567), [6569](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6569), [6570](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6570))

### Patron identity serves live traffic behind a toggle
- The new account service passed every sign-up and login test against FOLIO's test copy, and the account pages can now send linked patrons to it behind a switch that is still off. ([6850](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6850), [6785](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6785), [13908](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13908))
- The login fault is fixed ahead of the next rehearsal, and existing patrons can now join the early test group. ([6886](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6886), [6881](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6881))

### Synchronise data between Axiell and Folio
- Items sent to FOLIO now say how they can be ordered, follow Axiell when their access changes, and are hidden when staff withdraw them in Axiell. ([6862](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6862), [6834](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6834))
- The sync now points at FOLIO's test copy but has not run there yet, so that half of the sprint goal moves to Sprint 5. ([6849](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6849))

### Build a base for improving Image search
- The image search hackday's temporary services were removed on 16 November, as attendees had been told. ([6810](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6810))

### Other
- Every alert now names its service, severity and owner; of 70 that reached nobody, 19 now reach the team and 51 were removed. This and new records of change and service ownership answer part of a Wellcome service management review. ([6876](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6876), [6791](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6791), [6874](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6874))
- The catalogue can now sort works by title, ready for the archives browser. ([6853](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6853))
