# Progress against our quarterly objectives

## Digital Experience
### Highlights
-   The new brand colours went live across the website on 2 November, and matching changes to the sign-in pages and account emails were finished on 9 November.
-   On 9 November the image sidebar preview went public, so a picture's details in image search now open beside the results instead of covering them.
-   The same day, the archives browsing pages and the thematic browsing category pages opened to everyone. ([13519](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13519), [13529](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13529), [13776](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13776), [12745](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/12745))

### Deliver a faster, friction-free image browsing and viewing experience
-   The sidebar slides in beside the results, keeps your place in the grid and holds still for people who have turned off motion in their settings. ([13529](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13529), [13788](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13788))
-   The back button now steps through the images you looked at; the team's own first version always left the first image open, and the fix also cleared a stray "#" from the web address, reported in February 2025. ([13758](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13758), [11631](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/11631))
-   Staff tried the sidebar on desktop, tablet and mobile devices at a pop-up session on the Street, and the findings were written up. ([13705](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13705), [13784](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13784))

### Improve our agility to get working changes in front of users sooner
-   The sidebar's MVP (first phase) went public on 9 November, and the second, which lets people drag the sidebar wider, is built but not yet public. ([13537](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13537))
-   QR codes let test participants switch the sidebar on and off on their own devices; a test setting can hold someone on an older version after a newer one goes public, so the team will set up a routine for testing sessions. ([13713](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13713), [13717](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13717))

### Other
-   Archive collection pages now name the type of archive and link to its new browsing page.
-   Established baseline for new thematic browsing pages; visitors who already browse by theme view 4.85 works and subjects per visit. ([13382](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13382), [13215](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13215))
-   Implemented additional tracking for kiosk-mode. ([13819](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13819))
-   Every finding from a security scan was fixed, though one fix put the press releases page out of order until it was corrected the next day. ([576](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform-infrastructure/issues/576), [13793](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13793))
-   On 30 October the team blocked an automated program copying the site, which was making nearly a quarter of all requests and causing item pages to time out for visitors. ([13801](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13801))

## Digital Platform
### Highlights
FOLIO library records now merge with their digitised copies and images, so those stay on the right work page after the move off Sierra. Records deleted in Axiell Collections will be deleted from FOLIO too, as agreed with Collection Information, though the sync still writes only to a test system. And a security pass fixed over 1,000 warnings about vulnerable software and switched on scanning in all 12 of our cloud accounts, with daily digests and immediate alerts. No critical problem with a known fix remains on the services we run; the rest sit in Digirati's own systems. ([6580](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6580), [6438](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6438), [6833](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6833), [575](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform-infrastructure/issues/575))

### Folio works and items are transformed into the catalogue's Work model and merged correctly
- Items now carry their Sierra item numbers, so each can keep the identifier that saved links and future requests rely on. ([6614](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6614), [6616](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6616))
- About 3,400 works ordered but not yet catalogued still read "Awaiting cataloguing", online resources keep their links, and a few hundred records in larger sets show as hierarchies. ([6778](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6778), [6612](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6612), [6572](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6572))

### Patron identity serves live traffic behind a toggle
- The steps that create and update a patron's FOLIO record at sign-up and login now sit in the identity service, and test accounts use an email pattern agreed with CPL. Nothing serves patrons yet. ([6782](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6782), [6784](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6784), [6339](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6339), [6835](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6835), [6838](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6838))

### Synchronise data between Axiell and Folio
- The sync now finds its records by the Axiell record's own identifier and lets FOLIO number them. ([6805](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6805))

### Build a base for improving Image search
- Developers rehearsed the setup for the joint image search hackday on 3 and 4 November. ([6809](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6809))

### Other
- Short bursts of automated traffic were making some image requests fail for readers; that traffic is now blocked. ([6843](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6843))
