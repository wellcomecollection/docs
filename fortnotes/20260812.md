# Progress against our quarterly objectives

## Digital Experience
### Highlights
Archive browsing moved from design into build, with final designs shared with stakeholders and the collection search extended to support the new pages. And the new brand colours were applied across the whole site behind a staff-only switch, ready for design review. ([13220](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13220), [13007](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13007), [13209](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13209))

### Support researchers to access and engage with collections material online
Complete refactor of item viewer<br>
- The viewer was reorganised so each viewing scenario lives in its own clearly named place, easier to read and check. ([12985](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/12985))
- A readability pass tidied names and removed duplication, fixing one genuine fault found along the way, and safeguards now stop the old and new versions drifting apart. ([13329](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13329), [13332](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13332))

### Build an engaging collections browse experience which promotes discovery
- Final designs for making archive collections clear and inviting entry points were shared with stakeholders, and building began behind a staff-only switch. ([13007](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13007), [13317](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13317))
- The collection search API can now pick out archive collections, group them by type and s sort them in archive order, ready for the new pages. ([6469](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6469))
- The exhibition page's copy was finalised and visitor tracking put in place before launch. ([13272](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13272), [13348](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13348), [13220](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13220))
- The catalogue search API now offers archive filters and natural archive ordering behind a toggle. ([6469](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6469), [6509](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6509))

### Ensure the website reflects updated brand colours in a consistent and accessible way across all user journeys
- Every colour on the site can now be swapped to its nearest new brand equivalent with one staff-only switch, so the whole site can be reviewed at once. ([13207](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13207), [13209](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13209))
- Purple, turquoise and salmon have no equivalent in the new range and fall back to grey for now, flagged for design to resolve before anything goes public. ([13331](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13331))

### Other
- Web site sign-in software was brought up to date ([11705](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/11705))
- A broken "send a new verification email" button on account pages was traced and fixed. ([13314](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13314))

## Digital Platform
### Finish Axiell Collections catalogue integration
- The parallel system was cleared, every source run through it in full, and each difference from the live catalogue classified as expected, an improvement, or needing a fix. ([6461](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6461), [6445](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6445), [6465](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6465))
- 16,788 photographic records showed a superseded title as their title; the fault sat in a file we supply to Axiell, and the corrected version was installed and verified clean after a re-run. ([6473](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6473))
- Access wording, visibility, date and title-styling differences were written up with examples for collections staff; the date and title changes are signed off, several as improvements. ([6477](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6477), [6478](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6478), [6480](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6480))

### Synchronise data from AxC to Folio
- The feed was exercised end to end against a test copy of Folio, and its field mapping
- extended to cover what the cataloguing team needs. ([6441](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6441), [6462](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6462))

### New APIs for Identity and Requesting
- The Identifiers API design completed stakeholder review, and a companion decision on how preservation identifiers survive the library system migration was published. ([6402](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6402), [RFC](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/docs/pull/159))

### Other
- The 2018 editorial photography accession was transferred to permanent storage. ([6384](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6384))
