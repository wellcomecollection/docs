# Progress against our quarterly objectives

## Digital Experience
### Highlights
iPads are in Tenderness and Rage for visitors to use. This is a first for us as a team to have an online experience installed in the venue. <br>

Browsing the archives took a visible step forward. New pages listing archives by type went up behind a staff-only switch, and the catalogue now supplies the short descriptions and access details the designs call for. The rebuild of the item viewer, the part of the site people use to read digitised material, continued through its planned phases. And a browser check now stops automated traffic that had been loading item pages from hundreds of thousands of addresses a day and doubling the load on the image servers. ([13413](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13413), [12986](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/12986), [13419](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13419))

### Support researchers to access and engage with collections material online
- The item viewer rebuild moved on, with shared details now worked out once and reused rather than recalculated, and the new and old versions checked against each other throughout. ([12986](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/12986), [12987](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/12987))
- Staff with permission to view restricted material were seeing nothing for restricted thumbnails. They now see the images, while everyone else still sees the padlock. ([13437](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13437))

### Build an engaging collections browse experience which promotes discovery

The following are behind toggles:<br>
- New pages listing archives by type, sortable by date, are up behind a staff-only switch, with the archive card redesigned to match the latest designs. ([13413](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13413), [13323](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13323))
- The catalogue now supplies a one-sentence description for each archive and access details for every item in a collection, for the browse pages to use. ([6488](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6488), [6489](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6489))
- Every archive collection now has a one-sentence description on the public catalogue; across 993 collections, unusable boilerplate openings fell from 253 to 1. ([6488](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6488))
- Works now say how each item can be accessed (online, by request, on the shelves),in a form the browse designs can filter and count. ([6489](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6489), [6510](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6510))

### Implement new brand colours across the digital experience
- Two design concepts for applying the new colour palette were produced and presented to stakeholders, so a direction can be agreed before build begins. ([13210](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13210))

### Other
- The cookie policy overstated YouTube's privacy. Pages with videos were setting YouTube visitor cookies before anyone pressed play; nothing now loads until a video is played, and the policy describes what actually happens. ([13403](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13403))
- An issue with account pages has bee fixed ([13439](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13439))

## Digital Platform

### Highlights
The public catalogue now carries the fields the new archive browsing experience needs, clearing the way for the website team to build it. The parallel catalogue of migrated archive records was cleared and rebuilt a third time against the September data load, which confirmed a long-awaited correction: 9,848 records that should be closed to the public in migrated data will be when used on the public catalogue. And the image server gained defences against two waves of bulk downloading. ([6510](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6510), [6623](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6623), [527](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform-infrastructure/issues/527))

## Finish Axiell Collections catalogue integration
- The third full rebuild ran end to end with every record accounted for and a single  record still failing conversion; one step reported success while dropping 8,819 images, caught and recovered. ([6624](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6624))
- Checks on the new load found two known data problems still unfixed at source, undeleted reference guides and stray subject-heading markers, now reported back for fixing before the next load. ([6623](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6623))
- Manuscripts catalogued as several archive records but transcribed in one digital text can now all connect to it; before, a second identifier removed the manuscript from the catalogue. ([6506](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6506))

### New APIs for Identity and Requesting
- The new Identifiers API moved out of prototype: it now builds alongside the main catalogue API and has secure access to the registry it serves. ([6680](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6680), [970](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/catalogue-api/pull/970))
- How patron records are purged today, and how deletion should work after the library system migration, is diagrammed to inform the patron identity design. ([6422](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6422))

### Other
- An automated browser network loading item pages drove the image server to roughly double its normal traffic; limits and better alerting are in place. ([527](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform-infrastructure/issues/527), [539](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform-infrastructure/issues/539))
- An audio recording digitised in two parts had both online copies attached to one part; the fault was fixed, and a sweep found and corrected four more affected recordings. ([6643](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6643))
