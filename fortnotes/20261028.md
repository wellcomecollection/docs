# Progress against our quarterly objectives

## Digital Experience
### Highlights
- The new brand colours are ready to go live to the public after completing improved automated accessibility checks.
- Short descriptions for archive collections are now live on the site, displaying in search results and on archive collection level pages.
- The first slice of the new image search preview is built: choosing an image opens its details in a panel beside the results rather than covering them, and pop-up user testing with staff has been completed. ([13534](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13534), [13665](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13665), [13608](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13608))

### Deliver a faster, friction-free image browsing and viewing experience
- Designs for the first two slices of the preview were agreed, and a plan executed for testing the first with staff on phones and tablets. ([13626](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13626), [13666](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13666), [13658](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13658))
- The chosen image is highlighted in the results, keyboard users return to it, and choosing it again closes the panel, with animation and the back button still to do. ([13534](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13534), [13733](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13733))

### Build an engaging collections browse experience which promotes discovery
- Each archive type on the new archives browser page has new agreed descriptions and images, and two collections were removed: one returning to its depositor, one due to join another archive, with a final check to come before release. ([13645](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13645), [13642](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13642))

### Other
- A fault in the current image pop-up sent “View image” to the wrong page of a book; it is fixed in both versions, and single images lost a redundant second button. ([13694](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13694), [13627](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13627))
- To manage bot traffic, about 3,500 people a week had to try the puzzle and a quarter failed it. Our own cookie banner had also wiped the record of passing, so anyone who ignored the banner was checked on every page; that is fixed. ([13665](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13665), [13721](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13721))
- The new accessibility checks found one real contrast failure under the new colours, now fixed, and work pages now give screen readers valid language codes, a fault open since February 2025. ([13720](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13720), [11627](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/11627))

## Digital Platform
### Highlights
The sprint met its goal. A catalogue pipeline for FOLIO, the library system replacing Sierra, is running, and staff can preview it on the real website. The Identifiers API, which links catalogue items to FOLIO for requesting, is finished and documented for developers. And digitisation has an agreed way to identify items once Sierra is gone. ([6534](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6534), [6714](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6714), [6587](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6587), [6403](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6403), [6644](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6644), [6669](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6669))

### Folio works and items are transformed into the catalogue’s Work model and merged correctly
- FOLIO records now carry subjects, genres, contributors and lettering. ([6565](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6565), [6547](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6547))
- FOLIO items now say where they are kept and whether a reader can request them, under rules agreed this sprint, in the preview only. ([6608](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6608), [6589](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6589))
- FOLIO re-saved almost its whole collection three times in three days, which stopped our feed on 22 October. It caught up by 27 October and now splits large changes up. ([6824](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6824), [6812](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6812))

### Patron identity serves live traffic behind a toggle
- The new patron identity service is installed in development and staging, after the development login service moved onto the paid plan it needed. Nothing uses it yet. ([6599](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6599), [6600](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6600))

### Synchronise data between Axiell and Folio
- Synced items now record their Axiell storage locations and a loan type taken from the record instead of one fixed value. Switching the sync on, due with FOLIO’s 27 October go-live for Collections Information staff, waits on work carried into next sprint. ([6774](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6774), [switch-on](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6667))

### Define the final steps for Sierra’s retirement
- FOLIO’s record number will identify digitised items, reusing the b-number where there is one. The team will build the export into the digitisation workflow. ([RFC 091](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/docs/pull/176))

### Build a base for improving Image search
- For the 3 and 4 November hackday, a demo searches 123,505 images with a modern image model alongside today’s. ([6780](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6780))

### Other
- 5,236 catalogue records showing outdated names for people and subjects were refreshed, and later corrections now reach records by themselves. ([6764](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6764))
- At collections staff’s request, one “for deletion?” tick now hides an Axiell record online, and 83 born-digital works lost a misleading “Closed stores” location. ([6802](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6802), [6811](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6811))
