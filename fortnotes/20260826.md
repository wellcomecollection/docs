# Progress against our quarterly objectives

## Digital Experience
### Highlights
The archives browsing experience took shape behind a staff-only switch: a new page lists every type of archive we hold with a count of collections in each, and the page for an archive collection gained tabs and a contents table that works on a phone.

### Support researchers to access and engage with collections material online
- The Tenderness and Rage iPads were signed off; PDFs now open on them too, provided Google Docs is allowed in the kiosk app settings. ([13199](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13199), [13389](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13389), [13259](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13259))

### Build an engaging collections browse experience which promotes discovery
- All behind toggles:
- A "Browse archives" link now leads to a page listing 15 archive types, each with a count, drawn from a catalogue feed that is still in testing; the descriptions are placeholders awaiting real copy. ([13318](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13318), [13352](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13352))
- Archive collections are now marked as such in search results, and their pages gained About and Contents tabs and a contents table reworked for small screens ([13320](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13320), [13381](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13381), [13393](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13393))
- The selected tab is now kept in the web address, so the back button and shared links  return to it; the same work cured a long-standing fault that made tabs on What's On and concept pages unusable without JavaScript. ([13319](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13319), [10445](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/10445))

### Other
- Registering for a library account from a work page left visitors back on that page with placeholder initials instead of the confirmation screen ([13313](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13313))
- The item viewer was retrying failed thumbnail loads with a malformed request that could never succeed, about 59.4 million times a month; the retry no longer runs for thumbnails, and why so many thumbnails fail to load is being looked at separately. ([13395](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13395))

## Digital Platform

### Highlights
The second full load of archive records from Axiell Collections went through the new catalogue pipeline and was compared against the live catalogue. The load fixed what it set out to fix, with swapped titles down from 18,970 records to 230, but introduced two regressions of its own. We also have an agreed way to keep digitised archive material connected to its images once Sierra is retired, without which around 93% of it would lose them. ([6503](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6503), [6505](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6505), [6508](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6508), [6525](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6525))

### Finish Axiell Collections catalogue integration

Axiell records pass all the way through a parallel pipeline with new merge rules

- Only 3.3% of Axiell records cite the library number linking them to their digitised images. A matching file of 197,128 pairs is built and a 10-record sample checked; the full import follows. ([6525](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6525), [RFC 092](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/docs/pull/164))

Axiell records are fully transformed into the Work model
- Subject terms on 11,466 records arrived with "(LCSH)" baked into the text, splitting one subject into two. The marker is now stripped and every affected record re-processed. ([6577](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6577))
- Creation dates lost day and month precision on roughly 60,000 works, apparently because our request for year-only dates on name records was applied to the wrong field, and 9,305 closed items still display as Restricted. Both go back to Axiell. ([6576](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6576), [6574](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6574))
- Of the 88 records rejected in round one, 28 are fixed or removed; 52 born-digital files with no rights information wait on digital production. ([6472](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6472), [6493](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6493), [6526](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6526))

### Synchronise data from AxC to Folio
- The fields to be copied from Axiell to FOLIO are agreed with collections and library staff, bar two waiting on the library workstream. ([6251](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6251))

### New APIs for Identity and Requesting
- Library records are now identified by FOLIO's permanent identifiers rather than its human-readable numbers, which requesting depends on; the API design was corrected to match. ([6418](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6418), [6538](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6538))

### Other
- Two faults in how we put changes live were closed off: replacing the catalogue's access keys caused 280 visitor-facing errors, and unreviewed code briefly reached the live concepts service. ([6528](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6528), [6595](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6595))
- The preservation system stalled on a 1.5 GB digitised item and on the 2018 editorial photography transfer; both were fixed and its workers are now protected from being stopped mid-job. ([6517](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6517), [6518](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6518), [6500](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6500))
