# Progress against our quarterly objectives

## Digital Experience
### Highlights
The Archive Collection level pages toggle was made public.

The new archive collection pages are finished and through design review, showing a short summary of each archive on its page, card and search result, still behind a staff-only switch. The rebuilt item viewer is ready to be switched on for everyone, with its staff-only markers removed and its plan updated to match what was built. The final proposal for the new brand colours across page templates was agreed. ([13321](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13321), [13390](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13390), [13447](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13447), [plan](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/docs/pull/172), [13212](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13212))

### Support researchers to access and engage with collections material online
- Design sprint prototypes were tested with visitors through pop-up sessions in the Library. ([13418](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13418))

### Build an engaging collections browse experience which promotes discovery
- Archive category descriptions were rewritten in short and long versions. ([13476](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13476))
- Clicks on an archive's contents list are now counted in analytics, and the page is included in automated accessibility checks. ([13414](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13414), [13410](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13410))

### Implement new brand colours across the digital experience
- The agreed colours are applied to key page templates in the designs, with extra components flagged; the component library and live site come next. ([13212](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13212))

### Other
- Shared links to search pages now carry the right title and description, though the team's own bot challenge on those pages still hides it from some preview services. ([11660](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/11660), [13499](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13499))
- Automated test runs no longer count as visitors in analytics, and each visit's browser details are now recorded to help filter out bots. ([13392](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13392), [13037](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13037))
- Event pages dated after the clocks go back were turning into an error page in the latest Chrome; fixed and live before the clocks change on 25 October. ([fix](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13510))

## Digital Platform

### Highlights
The public catalogue is now using data from Axiell Collections, having switched over from using CALM data. Previously stakeholders signed off the archive records converted from Axiell Collections on 21 September, after final checks and the first structured testing by staff. The new catalogue was then rebuilt from Axiell's live data, ready for the website to switch to it. A new Identifiers API is live for internal services. ([6338](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6338), [6719](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6719), [6543](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6543))

### Finish Axiell Collections catalogue integration

### Axiell records are fully transformed into the Work model
- A final check of 204,197 archive records found closed access, missing last round, now matches the current catalogue. ([6642](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6642))
- Access notes contradicting themselves on 5,564 records were fixed, and a fault that lost 8,819 images without any alert now stops the run instead. ([6690](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6690), [6688](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6688))

### Axiell records pass all the way through a parallel pipeline with new merge rules
- The rebuild ran twice with a second run on 25 September. ([6718](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6718))
- Those numbers link 764 more digitised archive items back to their records. ([6719](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6719))
- The CALM feed was switched off once CALM went read-only, keeping a checked copy of all 408,710 records. ([6689](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6689))

### Synchronise data from AxC to Folio
- The sync copying archive items into FOLIO now runs on a schedule against a test FOLIO system, though checks that records arrive are not recorded yet. ([6696](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6696), [change](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/catalogue-pipeline/pull/3671))

### New APIs for Identity and Requesting
- The Identifiers API lets systems such as digitisation look up a record's catalogue number from its number elsewhere. ([6543](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6543), [6578](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6578))

### Other
- The new catalogue reads four more details from FOLIO library records, such as descriptions, ahead of FOLIO replacing Sierra. ([6546](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6546), [6553](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6553))
