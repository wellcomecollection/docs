# Progress against our quarterly objectives

## Digital Experience
### Highlights
The new brand colours have been applied across the site behind a staff-only switch, from page backgrounds, header and footer through cards, labels, buttons, tags and tabs to the viewer controls, the audio player and a new favicon, with a first round of design review fixes already in. The rebuilt item viewer is now the only version: the old one was deleted four weeks after the new one went public with nothing reported, and the switch between them has gone. ([13517](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13517), [12990](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/12990))

### Support researchers to access and engage with collections material online
- The rebuilt item viewer is now the only version: the old one deleted, the switch removed and the duplicate tests that ran both versions folded away, with further test improvements still in hand. ([12990](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/12990))

### Build an engaging collections browse experience which promotes discovery
- Collections Information approved the descriptions and images for the archive types browse page and asked for two types to be dropped, and for large archive trees the confusing "show 50 more rows" behaviour was agreed as the first fix, which needs a change to the catalogue API before it can be built. ([13538](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13538), [13539](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13539))

### Implement new brand colours across the digital experience
- Brand colours: components across eighteen tickets recoloured to the designs behind the switch and reviewed by design, with the first round of fixes applied; two tidy-ups follow, including how highlighted text reads on a pale yellow background. ([13517](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13517), [13562](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13562), [13615](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/issues/13615))

### Improve our agility to get working changes in front of users sooner
- Staff can now preview any switch they have starred from a floating control on the page they are looking at, without going back to the dashboard; a fault that wiped the selection when more than one switch was starred was fixed on 12 October. ([13507](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13507), [13635](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13635), [13636](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13636))
- The archive collection pages now run from a single staged switch rather than two separate ones, so each further step can go public on its own; the first step is already live for everyone. ([13513](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13513), [13533](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/wellcomecollection.org/pull/13533))

## Digital Platform
### Highlights
The public catalogue moved on 6 October to a pipeline that builds each archive tree from the parent link cataloguers pick in Axiell rather than a typed reference, and that carries the library's Backstage authority load, checked with Sierra updates paused from 1 to 6 October. Both earlier pipelines are now decommissioned. The Identifiers API is finished: tested to 400 requests a second with no cache needed, keyed per consumer and alarmed. The FOLIO work moved on every front below. ([6743](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6743), [6725](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6725), [6723](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6723), [6726](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6726), [6748](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6748), [6536](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6536), [6535](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6535), [6539](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6539))

### Folio -> public catalogue integration is ready for the switchover window
- Production, languages, genres, edition, physical description, designation and frequency now come through on FOLIO records, and series relations were checked against real FOLIO data. ([6566](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6566), [6768](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6768))
- Records whose FOLIO data names two different library numbers are held back, with 195 sent to Collections Information to fix, and 4,273 Axiell records that had been given fresh identifiers were repaired so their old addresses survive. ([6575](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6575), [6754](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6754))

### New APIs for Identity and Requesting
- The v2 identity API moved into the identity codebase with automated checks, then was hardened against account abuse and given proper error responses; nothing serves patrons yet. ([6597](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6597), [6759](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6759))

### Synchronise data between Axiell and Folio
- Items now take their home location rather than their current one, correcting a rule that sent Euston Road material to Deepstore, and a status from their access category instead of always "Available"; the feed was refreshed so 96.5% of records carry the location. ([6731](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6731), [3746](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/catalogue-pipeline/pull/3746))
- Only records that Collections Information have ticked for FOLIO are sent to it again, as the design requires; writes still go to a test system. ([6663](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6663))

### Other
- Wrong creator names, Bliss shown as someone else, came from a rule matching short names to any alias; it was tightened and labels made stable between rebuilds. ([6679](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6679), [6737](https://github.com/wellcomecollection/platform/issues/6737))
